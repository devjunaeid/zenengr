# US-064 — Cumulative statement invoice issuance & dual-portal PDF view

## Story

As a tenant admin, I want to click "Generate Invoice" to issue a cumulative statement invoice snapshotting the progressive charges, payments, and net due / advance balance into an official invoice document (`INV-XXXX`), so that both staff and client users can view and download the progressive invoice.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] "Generate Invoice" creates and immediately issues an official invoice (`INV-XXXX`) containing the chronological charge and payment line items. (FR-19.4)
- [ ] Generating subsequent invoices across different days incorporates previous charges, new services, payments, and the updated net due / advance balance. (FR-19.4)
- [ ] PDF rendering styles charges and payment credits cleanly in a single chronological table with due balance and advance credits. (FR-19.5)
- [ ] Issued statement invoices are listed and viewable in both Staff and Client Portals with PDF download support. (FR-19.6)

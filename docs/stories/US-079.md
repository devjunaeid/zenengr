# US-079 - Dynamic service price updates and persistent ledger balance consistency

## Story

As a project manager or admin,
I want to update the price of attached project services at any time during the project lifecycle when scope changes,
So that the project ledger automatically updates total project value, accurately subtracts all existing payments, and always presents a consistent balance due.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-079.1:** Service price can be updated at any time in the project timeline without being blocked by 409 Conflict when prior invoices exist.
- **AC-079.2:** When a service price is updated, the corresponding ledger charge updates to reflect the new price.
- **AC-079.3:** All past payment records (both direct project payments and invoice payments) remain intact and continue deducting from the updated project total.
- **AC-079.4:** The ledger summary calculations (`subtotal`, `total`, `paid`, `due`, `advance_balance`) always maintain mathematical consistency: `due = max(total - paid, 0)` and `advance_balance = max(paid - total, 0)`.
- **AC-079.5:** Payment summary calculation unifies all direct payments and invoice transactions, avoiding double-counting or omission.
- **AC-079.6:** Service price changes are audited with before/after values and actor details.

# US-019 — Client list with rollups, search, filter, tags, internal notes

## Story

As a staff member, I want a client list with summary rollups, search/filter, tags, and internal notes so I can quickly find clients and understand their status at a glance.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Client list shows summary rollups: number of active projects, total invoiced, total outstanding balance. (FR-5.4)
2. Staff can add free-text internal notes to a Client record; notes are invisible on Client Portal. (FR-5.5, AC-3)
3. Staff can add simple tags/labels to a Client record. (FR-5.5, AC-3)
4. Search by name returns matching clients. (FR-5.7, AC-5)
5. Filter by status, tag, and outstanding balance range. (FR-5.7, AC-5)
6. Notes are internal-only, never visible on Client Portal. (FR-5.5)

# US-038 — Client User views payment history and outstanding balance (view-only)

## Story

As a Client User, I want to view my payment history and outstanding balance so I can track what I've paid and what I still owe.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Client Portal shows payment history for the client's invoices. (FR-9.5, AC-6)
2. Payment history includes: transaction date, amount, method, reference. (FR-9.5)
3. Outstanding balance is displayed per invoice and as a client total. (FR-9.5, AC-6)
4. View is read-only — Client User cannot record or modify payments. (FR-9.5)
5. Only payments on the client's own invoices are visible (no cross-client visibility). (FR-4.8)

# US-018 — Staff create/edit/view Clients with contact and billing info

## Story

As a Tenant Admin or Manager, I want to create, edit, and view client records with contact details and billing info so I can manage who my organization works with.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Tenant Admin/Manager can create a Client with name, contact info, billing address, tax ID, and initial status (Active/Archived). (FR-5.1, FR-5.2)
2. Tenant Admin/Manager can edit Client contact and billing fields. (FR-5.1)
3. A Client can have multiple Client Users (contacts) with a single primary billing contact. (FR-5.3)
4. Employee role has view-only access to Clients. (FR-4.2)
5. Client record shows status (Active/Archived). (FR-5.2)

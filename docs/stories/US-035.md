# US-035 — Staff record a payment transaction; invoice status auto-updates (Partially Paid / Paid)

## Story

As a Tenant Admin or Manager, I want to record a payment against an invoice so the system auto-updates the invoice status.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Tenant Admin/Manager can record a Transaction (payment) against an Issued invoice: amount, date, method (bank transfer, card, cash, other), reference note, recorded-by user. (FR-9.1, AC-1)
2. Recording a full payment (amount >= total) sets invoice status to Paid. (FR-9.2, AC-1)
3. Recording a partial payment (amount < total) sets invoice status to Partially Paid. (FR-9.2, AC-2)
4. Multiple partial payments accumulate; invoice becomes Paid when sum >= total. (AC-3)
5. Payment recording is logged in the audit trail. (FR-4.13)
6. Once recorded, a transaction cannot be edited or deleted — corrections via new records. (FR-9.6, AC-7)
